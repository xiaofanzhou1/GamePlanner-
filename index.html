Welcome to GamePlanner+!

GamePlanner+ is a platform for managing and discovering video games. It allows users to organize their game library, search for games and interact with a chatbot that recommends games based on their preferences, interests and gaming history.

Features

What can you do at GamePlanner+ ?

Manage your library

Create Collections and organize your games by category

Track your Sessions

Log hours played, dates and progress for each game.

Discover Games

Search new titles and get recommendations from the chatbot.

How it works

Get started with GamePlanner+ following this easy 3 steps.

Create a Collection

Set up collections to organize your games by category.

Add Games.

Search for games and add them to your library

Track Your Progress

Log play sessions and keep track of your progress over time.
